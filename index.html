About

The story

Nick Jacob is a singer, songwriter and musician based in Southern California. Over the past 35 years, Nick has written hundreds of songs spanning various genres from rock to folk. He co-wrote his first song, “Transaction”, a Run DMC style rap number with his friend Larry Bagby when they were both in fifth grade. The duo continued writing folk-inspired rock songs together in high school with catchy melodies and harmonies reminiscent of Crosby, Stills and Nash and The Eagles. In their early twenties, they formed a short-lived ’50s style rock ’n’ roll band called The Enchantments where Nick wrote some of the songs that would later appear on his 2018 debut solo album, Midnight Ride.

Nick's songwriting style contains a strong pop sensibility that employs catchy melodies, memorable hooks and thoughtful lyrics. While he personally enjoys music from almost every genre, many of his songs draw inspiration from the American music scenes of the 1950s, 1960s and 1970s. Currently, Nick is recording a new album, Ebb & Flow, that will be published in early 2027.
